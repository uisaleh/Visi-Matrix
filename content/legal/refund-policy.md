# Refund policy
Last updated: July 15, 2026. We want you to be confident trying VisiMatrix. This policy explains when refunds apply to subscriptions and credit purchases.
## 1. Try before you pay
The Free plan includes a project and starter credits with no card required, so you can evaluate the platform fully before subscribing. We encourage you to test on the Free plan first — it is the best way to know the service fits your needs.
## 2. Our payment processor
All purchases are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, our payment provider and the Merchant of Record for every order. Paddle collects payment, handles applicable sales tax, and issues refunds on our behalf under this policy. Paddle’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to your purchase.
## 3. Subscription refund windows
- Monthly plans: request a refund within 3 days of your payment (first purchase or renewal). We deduct the value of any credits already used in that billing cycle and refund the remaining balance.
- Yearly plans: request a refund within 14 days of your payment (first purchase or renewal). We deduct the value of any credits already used in that billing cycle and refund the remaining balance.
- Requests made after these windows are not eligible for a refund, whether for a first purchase or a renewal.
- Cancelling stops future charges; your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
## 4. Add-on credit purchases
Unused add-on credits are refundable within 3 days of purchase. Credits that have been spent on scans are not refundable, since the underlying AI provider costs have already been incurred.
## 5. Service issues
If a platform fault causes a scan to fail, the reserved credits are automatically released back to your balance. If you believe you were charged credits for a failed scan, contact support and we will investigate and re-credit where appropriate.
## 6. How to request a refund
Email support@visimatrix.com from the address on your account with your workspace name and the reason for the request. We respond within 2 business days. Once approved, the refund is issued by Paddle.com Market Ltd to your original payment method, typically arriving within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
## 7. Exceptions
We may decline refunds in cases of abuse, such as repeated purchase-and-refund cycles or violations of our terms & conditions. Statutory rights in your jurisdiction are not affected by this policy.
