# Privacy policy
Last updated: July 15, 2026. This policy explains what data VisiMatrix ("VisiMatrix", "we", "us") collects when you use our website and platform, and how we handle it.
## 1. Information we collect
We collect the following categories of information:
- Account information — your name, email address, password (stored hashed), and workspace name when you register.
- Project data — the websites, brand names, competitors, and prompts you configure for tracking.
- Usage data — scan history, credit transactions, and interactions with the platform, used to operate and improve the service.
- Billing information — payment details are collected and processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, our payment provider and the Merchant of Record for your purchase; we do not store full card numbers ourselves.
## 2. How we use your information
- To provide the service: running scans, computing visibility scores, and showing results.
- To manage your account, workspace membership, and billing.
- To send transactional emails such as receipts, scan notifications, and security alerts.
- To improve the platform through aggregated, de-identified usage analysis.
- To comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse of the service.
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
## 3. AI provider queries
To measure your visibility, we send the prompts you configure to third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, and Perplexity). These prompts describe market questions — they do not include your personal account information. Responses are stored so we can score and display them to you.
## 4. Cookies and local storage
We use strictly necessary cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your workspace selection. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.
## 5. Data sharing
We share data only with:
- Service providers that host our infrastructure, bound by contract.
- Paddle.com Market Ltd, our payment processor and Merchant of Record, to process subscription and credit purchases and issue refunds.
- AI providers, limited to the prompts described in section 3.
- Authorities, when required by law.
## 6. Data retention
We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a project, its scan data is deleted. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example, billing records).
## 7. Security
We protect your data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, and the principle of least privilege. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information.
## 8. Your rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@visimatrix.com.
## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by email or an in-app notice before they take effect. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
## 10. Contact
Questions about this policy or our data practices: support@visimatrix.com.
